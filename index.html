Privacy Policy and Support – Trauma Imaging Guide Privacy Policy Trauma Imaging Guide does not collect, persistently store, transmit, or share personal information or health data. Clinical checklist selections are held temporarily in the app’s memory. The app does not require user accounts and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking. Its reference pathways work offline. The app includes an optional link to an external PECARN calculator on MDCalc. Opening this link requires an internet connection. The external website operates under its own privacy policy. The app does not send checklist selections to that website. Clinical Use Trauma Imaging Guide is intended as a reference for trained healthcare professionals. It does not replace a complete patient assessment, independent clinical judgment, consultation with the responsible clinician, or applicable clinical protocols. Users should verify recommendations before making medical decisions. Support Trauma Imaging Guide is independently developed and maintained by Paul Kupicha.
